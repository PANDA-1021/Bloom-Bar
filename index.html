Who we are

Bloom Bar is a creative business that specialises in making handmade flowers using paper.

We started this business to offer a beautiful alternative to fresh flowers while exploring our creativity and providing customers with something unique.

Our flowers are designed to be attractive, affordable and long-lasting, making them perfect for different occasions.

Our mission

To create beautiful, affordable and long-lasting paper flowers that offer a creative alternative to fresh flowers.

Our vision

To become a creative flower brand known for unique handmade designs, quality and customer satisfaction.

From paper to petals

Our flowers go through a simple creative process. Every flower is carefully handmade with creativity and attention to detail.

  1. PreparationGather the paper and other materials needed.
  2. DesigningChoose the flower type, colours and design.
  3. CuttingCarefully cut the paper into the required shapes.
  4. CraftingShape and assemble the petals to create each flower.
  5. FinishingAdd the final details to complete the flower.
